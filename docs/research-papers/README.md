# Research Papers

This section contains notes and summaries of research papers I've read.

## Papers

### 2024

- [Computational Role of Structure in Neural Activity and Connectivity](./Ostojic_&_Fusi_2024) - Ostojic, S., & Fusi, S. (2024)
  - A review paper outlining a unified approach to characterize structure in neural activity and connectivity by inspecting representational geometry and modularity properties.

### 2021

- [Neural Population Geometry: An Approach for Understanding Biological and Artificial Neural Networks](./Chung_&_Abbott_2021) - Chung, S., & Abbott, L. F. (2021)
  - A review paper proposing neural population geometry as a common framework for understanding both biological and artificial neural networks.
